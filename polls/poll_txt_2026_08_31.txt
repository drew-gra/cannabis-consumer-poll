NuggMD Cannabis Consumer Poll
August 27, 2026 - August 31, 2026
Sample: 596 respondents | Margin of error: ±4.01%


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════


QUESTION 1: If medical cannabis could be mailed straight to your door, would that change how you shop?
(n=596)


RESULTS:
31% - Yes, because there isn't a dispensary within a reasonable distance of where I live
24% - Yes, even though there's a local dispensary, health or mobility issues make it hard for me to get there
17% - No, I prefer buying in person
7%  - No, I'd have privacy or security concerns about receiving it by mail
21%  - Not sure, I'd want more details (cost, packaging, delivery time) first


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════


METHODOLOGY:
Digital questionnaire sent to verified, representative sample of cannabis consumers in state-legal markets. Targeted sampling from verified consumer database with multi-point validation including consumption verification, attention checks, and statistical outlier detection.


SAMPLE DETAILS:
- Population: Representative sample of cannabis consumers in state-legal markets
- Survey Method: Digital questionnaire via secure online platform
- Confidence Level: 95%
- Z-score: 1.96
