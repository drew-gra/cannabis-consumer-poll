NuggMD Cannabis Consumer Poll
August 20, 2026 - August 24, 2026
Sample: 527 respondents | Margin of error: ±4.27%


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════


QUESTION 1: Are you aware of the testing standards for products sold at locations other than licensed dispensaries?
(n=527)


RESULTS:
20% - Yes, I check product testing before buying 
27% - No, I assume it is tested/regulated 
26% - No, I assume it isn’t tested/regulated 
8%  - I don’t care, it doesn't change what I buy 
20%  - I never thought about it 


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════


METHODOLOGY:
Digital questionnaire sent to verified, representative sample of cannabis consumers in state-legal markets. Targeted sampling from verified consumer database with multi-point validation including consumption verification, attention checks, and statistical outlier detection.


SAMPLE DETAILS:
- Population: Representative sample of cannabis consumers in state-legal markets
- Survey Method: Digital questionnaire via secure online platform
- Confidence Level: 95%
- Z-score: 1.96
