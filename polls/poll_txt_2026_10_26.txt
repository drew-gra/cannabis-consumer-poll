QUESTION 1: Have you ever gotten the giggles at an inconvenient moment?
(n=683)


RESULTS:
21% - Yes, all the time. I’ve made peace with it.
26% - Yes, a few times, and usually right when I needed to look serious.
8% - Yes, once, and I still cringe thinking about it.
7% - Almost, but I bit my lip and pulled it together.
19%  - No, my giggles only come out around people who’d laugh too.
19%  - Cannabis doesn’t usually make me laugh.

METHODOLOGY:
Digital questionnaire sent to verified, representative sample of cannabis consumers in state-legal markets. Targeted sampling from verified consumer database with multi-point validation including consumption verification, attention checks, and statistical outlier detection.


SAMPLE DETAILS:
- Population: Representative sample of cannabis consumers in state-legal markets
- Survey Method: Digital questionnaire via secure online platform
- Confidence Level: 95%
- Z-score: 1.96
