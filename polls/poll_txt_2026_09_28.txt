NuggMD Cannabis Consumer Poll
September 24, 2026 - September 28, 2026
Sample: 1005 respondents | Margin of error: ±3.09%


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════


QUESTION 1: How much has cannabis replaced your alcohol consumption?
(n=1005)


RESULTS:
32% - I have completely replaced alcohol with cannabis.
26% - It has greatly decreased my alcohol consumption.
8% - It has somewhat decreased my alcohol consumption.
10% - It has not changed my alcohol consumption.
2%  - It has somewhat increased my alcohol consumption.
3%  - It has greatly increased my alcohol consumption.
20% - I don't drink alcohol, so this doesn't apply to me.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════


METHODOLOGY:
Digital questionnaire sent to verified, representative sample of cannabis consumers in state-legal markets. Targeted sampling from verified consumer database with multi-point validation including consumption verification, attention checks, and statistical outlier detection.


SAMPLE DETAILS:
- Population: Representative sample of cannabis consumers in state-legal markets
- Survey Method: Digital questionnaire via secure online platform
- Confidence Level: 95%
- Z-score: 1.96
