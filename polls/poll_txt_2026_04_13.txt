NuggMD Cannabis Consumer Poll
March 12 - April 13, 2026
Sample: 534 respondents | Margin of error: ±4.24%

QUESTION 1: Which best describes your relationship with home cannabis cultivation?
(n=534)

RESULTS:
13% - I currently grow cannabis at home
28% - I have grown cannabis at home in the past
13% - I have never grown, but plan to start
24% - I have never grown, but I would if barriers were removed
22% - I have never grown and have no interest in doing so

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

QUESTION 2: What are the barriers preventing you from growing cannabis at home?

NOTE: Asked only to respondents who answered "I have never grown, but I would if barriers were removed" to Question 1. Multi-select; percentages are based on 129 respondents and do not sum to 100%.
(129 of 534 respondents answered this conditional question)

RESULTS:
78% - Legal uncertainty
13% - Weather
37% - Lack of space
31% - Complexity
42% - Startup costs
22% - The time commitment required
26% - Product might not be good
5% - Other

QUESTION 5: Which best describes how you approach cannabis purchasing?
(n=534)

METHODOLOGY:
Digital questionnaire sent to verified, representative sample of cannabis consumers in state-legal markets. Targeted sampling from verified consumer database with multi-point validation including consumption verification, attention checks, and statistical outlier detection.

SAMPLE DETAILS:
- Population: Representative sample of cannabis consumers in state-legal markets
- Survey Method: Digital questionnaire via secure online platform
- Confidence Level: 95%
- Z-score: 1.96
