QUESTION 1: When do you stock up on cannabis for Thanksgiving weekend?
(n=560)


RESULTS:
14.3% - The day before Thanksgiving (Green Wednesday).
18.0% - Earlier in the week.
15.0% - I wait for Black Friday deals.
52.7% - I don’t stock up. I buy on my normal schedule.
