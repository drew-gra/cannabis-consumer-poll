NuggMD Cannabis Consumer Poll
November 5, 2026 - November 9, 2026
Sample: 682 respondents | Margin of error: ±3.75%

QUESTION 1: More than 900 medical dispensaries are now registered with the DEA, putting them under federal rules for security, recordkeeping, and sourcing. Do you care if your dispensary is federally registered?
(n=682)


RESULTS:
36.7% - I care a lot
15.7% - I care a little
17.6% - I don't really care
11.6% - I don't care at all
18.4%  - I'm not sure yet

METHODOLOGY:
Digital questionnaire sent to verified, representative sample of cannabis consumers in state-legal markets. Targeted sampling from verified consumer database with multi-point validation including consumption verification, attention checks, and statistical outlier detection.


SAMPLE DETAILS:
- Population: Representative sample of cannabis consumers in state-legal markets
- Survey Method: Digital questionnaire via secure online platform
- Confidence Level: 95%
- Z-score: 1.96
