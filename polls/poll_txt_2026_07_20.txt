NuggMD Cannabis Consumer Poll
July 16 - July 20, 2026
Sample: 583 respondents | Margin of error: ±4.06%

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

QUESTION 1: Several states have just banned hemp-derived THC products like THCA. How have these bans affected your access to cannabis?
(n=583)

RESULTS:
50% - I got my medical cannabis card
6% - I've switched to licensed dispensaries
8% - I've had trouble finding products
20% - No impact
8% - I don't use hemp-derived products
8% - I was not aware of these state changes

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

METHODOLOGY:
Digital questionnaire sent to verified, representative sample of cannabis consumers in state-legal markets. Targeted sampling from verified consumer database with multi-point validation including consumption verification, attention checks, and statistical outlier detection.

SAMPLE DETAILS:
- Population: Representative sample of cannabis consumers in state-legal markets
- Survey Method: Digital questionnaire via secure online platform
- Confidence Level: 95%
- Z-score: 1.96
